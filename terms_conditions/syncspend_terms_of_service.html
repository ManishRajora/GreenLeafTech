7. Group Features, Ownership, and Member Rules

7.1 Nature of Group Expense Records

SyncSpend's group feature is designed as an expense logging tool for funds administered by the group owner. Expense entries recorded within a group represent logs of expenditures made from funds under the group owner's administration. Members who create entries within a group are recording how the group owner's administered funds were spent. Group expense entries may contain personal data contributed by members, processed jointly for the purpose of shared financial record-keeping. Users acknowledge that participation in a group involves shared data control between the group owner and members within the boundaries described in this Section. Accordingly, group expense records are primarily the administrative records of the group owner.

7.2 Group Owner Rights and Responsibilities

  • The group owner has exclusive rights to create, manage, configure, and permanently delete the group and all data within it.
  • The group owner may add or remove members at any time without notice or explanation.
  • The group owner can view all expense entries logged by all current and former members within their groups.
  • The group owner is solely responsible for the accuracy of group financial records and for any decisions made on the basis of those records.
  • When a group owner permanently deletes a group, all associated group data including expense entries, settlement records, and member records are permanently and irrecoverably erased from cloud servers and locally cached copies. This action cannot be undone. The group owner bears sole responsibility for exporting any required data before deleting the group.
  • The group owner is responsible for ensuring that all group members are informed of and agree to these Terms before being added to any group.

7.3 Member Conduct and Limitations

  • Group members may log expense entries within groups they have been added to by the group owner.
  • A member may edit or delete their most recently submitted expense entry within a group, provided no subsequent expense entry has been submitted by any member after their entry. Once any subsequent entry exists, the earlier entry is permanently locked and cannot be edited or deleted. This rule exists to preserve the integrity of the sequential financial record.
  • Members may not edit, delete, or alter any expense entry submitted by another member.
  • Members may not attempt to circumvent the entry locking mechanism through any technical or other means.
  • Members acknowledge that their expense entries, once locked, form part of a permanent financial record owned and administered by the group owner.

7.4 Member Removal

  • When a member is removed from a group, they immediately and permanently lose the ability to view, access, add, or modify any data within that group.
  • Expense entries previously submitted by a removed member remain within the group record permanently for the group owner's record-keeping purposes. The display name associated with those entries is retained as it appeared at the time of submission.
  • A removed member has no right to demand deletion of their expense entries from a group record, as those entries form part of the group owner's financial log. GreenLeafTech will assess any such requests under the limitations described in the Privacy Policy.

7.5 Group Dissolution

Upon deletion of a group by the group owner, all group data is permanently and irrecoverably destroyed. Neither the group owner nor any member may recover any data from a deleted group. GreenLeafTech bears no liability for any loss arising from group deletion.
