Feature 06 — Settlements

The problem we're solving

Splitting expenses is easy.
Collecting money without tension is the hard part.

"After a group trip, we had to go through every receipt and chat message to figure out who owed what. It took three days and someone ended up feeling shortchanged regardless."

Settlements give every group expense a clean, verifiable ending. Both owner and member agree on the numbers before anything is finalised. No disputes. No "I don't remember that." No awkward follow-ups — just three steps that close the books permanently.

The 3-step process that eliminates disputes
✅
Step 1 — Owner initiatesTap "Initiate Settlement" on a member's screen. SyncSpend instantly calculates: Budget allocated minus Total spent = amount to return or receive. Sent to the member immediately.
🕐
Step 2 — Member confirms figuresThe member reviews the numbers — budget, total spent, final amount. They tap "Confirm the Figures." Both parties have now agreed in-app. Settlement expires in 48 hours if the member doesn't respond.
🔒
Step 3 — Owner closes and locksOnce confirmed, the owner taps "Close Settlement & Lock Expenses." The record is permanently sealed — no edits possible. Moves to Settlement History as a permanent, tamper-proof record.

Why the two-confirmation system matters

Neither party can dispute the final number — both literally tapped agree. The expense history is then locked permanently. This turns shared expenses from a recurring source of tension into a source of trust you can build on.

"Expenses locked" — what this means for you.

Once closed, no figure can be changed by anyone. Both parties have a sealed, agreed record. This single feature eliminates 100% of post-settlement disputes. Because there's nothing left to dispute.
