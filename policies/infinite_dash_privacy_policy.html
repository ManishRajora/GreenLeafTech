1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how GreenLeafTech ("we", "us", "our", "Developer") handles data in connection with the mobile application Infinite Dash ("App", "Game") available on the Google Play Store.

By downloading, installing, or playing the Game, you ("User", "you") acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the Game.

This Policy applies worldwide to all users of the Game regardless of country of residence. Where local laws impose stricter requirements, we endeavour to meet those requirements to the extent applicable.

This Policy is designed to comply with the Google Play Developer Programme Policy, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. About GreenLeafTech and This App

GreenLeafTech is the developer and data controller responsible for Infinite Dash. The Game is a free, fully offline endless runner game with multiple powerups, hurdles, and enemies spawning, where players aim for the highest score.

The Game is free to play, requires no user registration or account, makes no internet or network connections, contains no advertisements, and includes no in-app purchases. GreenLeafTech develops multiple applications, each governed by its own separate privacy policy. This Policy applies exclusively to Infinite Dash.

3. Information We Collect

We collect no personal data. Infinite Dash does not collect, record, transmit, or store any personal information, device identifiers, usage statistics, or analytics data of any kind. The Game operates entirely offline on your device.

3.1 Data Collected by GreenLeafTech

GreenLeafTech collects no data whatsoever from users of this Game. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no backend servers associated with this Game. No data is transmitted from the Game to GreenLeafTech or any third party.

3.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores the following data exclusively on your local device, which never leaves your device and is never accessible to GreenLeafTech:

  • High score and game progress
  • In-game settings and preferences (e.g. sound on/off)

This data is stored in the App's private internal storage, sandboxed by Android and not accessible to other applications. It is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Game.

3.3 Data Handled by Google Play Services (Platform Level)

The Game is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google Play Services and Play Integrity — which are part of the Android platform and are not SDKs integrated by GreenLeafTech — may handle minimal technical data at the platform level for app installation, licence verification, and integrity checking. This processing is performed entirely by Google LLC independently of GreenLeafTech, and GreenLeafTech does not receive, access, or control any such data. For details, refer to Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

3.4 Android Permissions

The Game requests no sensitive Android permissions. No permissions that access personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage are requested or used.

The Game does not request INTERNET, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, READ_CONTACTS, CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, AD_ID, or any biometric permission.

4. How We Use Your Data

Because GreenLeafTech collects no data from users of this Game, there is no data for us to use, process, or share. The Game functions entirely on your device without any data being transmitted to or processed by GreenLeafTech or any third-party service.

Local device data (high scores and settings) is used solely by the Game on your device to deliver the gameplay experience. GreenLeafTech has no access to this data.

5. Advertising

The Game does not contain any advertisements. GreenLeafTech does not work with any advertising network in connection with this Game, and no advertising-related data (such as the Android Advertising ID) is collected or used. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Policy will be updated in advance, users will be notified via an in-app notice, and any required consents will be sought before ads are served.

7. How We Share Your Data

GreenLeafTech does not collect any data from users of this Game and therefore does not share, sell, rent, trade, or disclose any personal data to any third party for any purpose.

GreenLeafTech does not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA or any other applicable law. There is no data sharing for advertising, marketing, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA and Families Policy)

Infinite Dash is a general-audience endless runner game intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13 as a primary audience and is not enrolled in the Google Play Designed for Families programme. Our Target Audience and Content (TACO) selection in the Google Play Console is set to ages 13 and above, consistent with this Policy.

8.1 Minimum Age Requirements

  • India (DPDP Act 2023): Users must be at least 18 years of age. Users between 13 and 17 years of age in India may use the Game only with verifiable parental or guardian consent, in accordance with the DPDP Act 2023.
  • All Other Jurisdictions: Users must be at least 13 years of age, in compliance with COPPA and applicable regional laws.

8.2 Visual Style and Child Appeal

GreenLeafTech acknowledges that endless runner games with colourful visuals may have incidental appeal to younger audiences. However, the Game is designed and marketed as a general-audience product and is not targeted at children under 13 as its primary or intended audience. This determination is reflected in our Google Play Console TACO questionnaire and content rating submission. If Google Play or any regulatory authority determines that the Game's actual audience includes a significant proportion of children under 13, GreenLeafTech will promptly review and update this Policy and any related data practices accordingly.

8.3 COPPA Compliance

Because the Game is fully offline and collects no data of any kind, there is no personal information being collected from any user — including children. The Game is inherently safe from a data-collection standpoint: no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, and no network connections mean there is no mechanism by which any child's personal information could be collected or transmitted.

8.4 If You Have Concerns About a Child Using the App

Because no data is collected, there is no data associated with any user's device that GreenLeafTech holds or could delete on request. If you have any concern about a child's use of this Game, you may contact us at greenleaftech.co.in@gmail.com with the subject line "Child Privacy Concern — Infinite Dash" and we will respond within 3 business days.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

9.1 Retention by GreenLeafTech

GreenLeafTech retains no data from users of this Game. There are no servers, databases, or data stores maintained by GreenLeafTech that hold any user data.

9.2 Local Data on Your Device

Game progress (high scores) and settings are stored only on your local device. You may delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data via your Android device settings (Settings → Apps → Infinite Dash → Storage → Clear Data) or by uninstalling the Game.

9.3 Data Deletion Rights

Because GreenLeafTech holds no user data, there is no data for us to delete on request. Nevertheless, we fully respect your rights under applicable law. If you believe any data has been collected in connection with your use of this Game and wish to submit a deletion request, please contact us at greenleaftech.co.in@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request — Infinite Dash". We will acknowledge your request within 7 days and respond fully within 30 days.

10. Data Security

Because the Game is fully offline and collects no personal data, the risk surface from a data security perspective is minimal. The following safeguards apply to local data stored on your device:

  • All local game data (scores, settings) is stored in the App's private internal storage, sandboxed by Android and inaccessible to other applications on your device.
  • On devices running Android 6.0 and above, this storage benefits from Android's application sandboxing and, where enabled by the device, file-system level encryption.
  • The Game makes no network connections, which means no data is ever transmitted over the internet — eliminating the risk of interception in transit entirely.
  • GreenLeafTech maintains no servers or databases associated with this Game, eliminating server-side data breach risk entirely.

In the event that any future update introduces network functionality, this Policy will be updated to describe the applicable security measures, including encryption standards, before the update is released.

13. GDPR — Rights for EEA and UK Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR applies. Because GreenLeafTech does not collect or process any personal data from users of this Game, no GDPR legal basis for processing is applicable to GreenLeafTech's own activities.

13.1 Your GDPR Rights

You retain the following rights under the GDPR. While GreenLeafTech holds no personal data to act upon, we honour these rights in full:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request confirmation of whether we hold any personal data about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of any inaccurate data we may hold.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of any personal data we hold.
  • Right to Restriction (Article 18): Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Receive any data we hold in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to any processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local supervisory authority (e.g. UK ICO at ico.org.uk, or your national EEA authority).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at greenleaftech.co.in@gmail.com with the subject line "GDPR Rights Request — Infinite Dash". We will respond within 30 days.

15. DPDP Act 2023 — Rights for Indian Users

GreenLeafTech is incorporated in India and is subject to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) and its implementing rules.

15.1 Our Role as Data Fiduciary

GreenLeafTech is the Data Fiduciary in respect of this Game. Because the Game collects no personal data, GreenLeafTech does not currently process any personal data as a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act in connection with this Game. Google LLC (as the provider of Google Play Services) independently handles platform-level data as a separate Data Fiduciary.

15.2 Consent

Because no personal data is collected, no consent for data processing is required from users of this Game under the DPDP Act. If a future update introduces data collection, this Policy will be updated and appropriate consent mechanisms will be implemented before any such data is collected.

15.3 Your Rights as a Data Principal

  • Right to Information: The right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed. GreenLeafTech processes no personal data from this Game.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure: The right to correct or erase personal data. GreenLeafTech holds no personal data to correct or erase.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: The right to have grievances addressed by our designated Grievance Officer within the timeframe prescribed by the DPDP Act.
  • Right to Nominate: The right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights under the DPDP Act on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
