Account Creation and Authentication

To use SyncSpend cloud features, users must create an account using:

  • Email and password
  • Supported third-party authentication methods

Authentication is handled securely by trusted third-party services such as Firebase Authentication or Supabase Authentication. We do not store or manage user passwords directly. Account creation and basic usage may be offered free of charge for an initial number of users, with limits subject to future change.

Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Email address (required for account creation)
  • Name or display name (optional)

2.2 Expense and Usage Data

  • Personal expense data entered by the user
  • Group expense data, including amounts, descriptions, dates, and members
  • Group and settlement information

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device and operating system information
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash reports and performance logs

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where applicable, we process user data based on consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations under GDPR.

Personal vs Group Expense Data

Personal Expense Data

  • Stored locally on the user’s device
  • Not uploaded to cloud servers unless cloud features are enabled

Group Expense Data

  • Stored securely in cloud databases (Firebase or Supabase)
  • Used solely for group sharing, synchronization, and settlement

Data Retention and Deletion Policy

SyncSpend follows a data minimization approach and retains user data only as long as necessary to deliver its services.

Personal Expense Data

  • Stored locally and under the user’s control
  • Not uploaded to SyncSpend servers unless cloud features are enabled

Group Expense Data

  • Stored securely in cloud databases
  • Free users retain access for up to 1 month
  • Paid users retain up to 6 months of data: 3 months of live editable data plus 3 months of archived read-only data

Permanent Deletion

  • Group expense data older than 180 days is permanently deleted
  • Deleted data cannot be recovered
  • Users must maintain their own records until a data export feature is available

Subscriptions and Payments

SyncSpend offers optional subscription plans for extended cloud storage and premium features.

  • Payments are processed securely through the Google Play Store
  • We do not collect or store payment card or banking details
  • Subscription status enables feature access and data retention limits

Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for marketing purposes. Third-party providers help us operate core features:

  • Google Firebase – authentication, cloud database, analytics, crash reporting
  • Supabase – authentication and cloud database services
  • Google Play Billing – subscription and payment processing

These providers process data per their privacy policies and applicable laws.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

SyncSpend may use cookies or similar technologies by service providers for analytics and performance monitoring, as outlined in those services' privacy documentation.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest when supported
  • Role-based access controls and secure authentication

Despite these measures, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • The right to request deletion of your account and cloud data
  • The right to restrict or object to certain processing
  • The right to data portability, where technically feasible

Requests can be made through the app or by contacting us at the details below.

International Data Transfers

Data processed by third-party providers may transfer across borders as needed for service delivery, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Children’s Privacy

SyncSpend is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy
