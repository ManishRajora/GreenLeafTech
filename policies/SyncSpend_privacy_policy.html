4. How Data is Stored - Personal vs. Group Expenses

SyncSpend operates on a clear separation between personal expense data and group expense data. Understanding this distinction is essential for understanding your data rights and responsibilities.

4.1 Personal Expense Data

Personal expense data refers to all expense entries created by a user for their own individual tracking purposes, outside of any group context.

  • Personal expense data is stored exclusively on the user's local device.
  • Personal expense data is stored exclusively on your local device and is not uploaded to SyncSpend's cloud infrastructure as part of ordinary personal expense tracking. Data associated with a shared group, which you join or create voluntarily, is synced as described in Section 4.
  • Personal expense data remains entirely under the user's control at all times.
  • Personal expense data and locally cached group data are stored in a local SQLite database on your device managed by the App. This database is private to the App and is not accessible to other applications on the device. This database is protected by Android's application sandbox and the operating system's security mechanisms. SyncSpend does not independently encrypt this database beyond the protection provided by the device and Android platform.
  • If the user uninstalls the App from their device, all personal expense data stored locally is permanently and irrecoverably lost. GreenLeafTech has no ability to recover this data. Users are solely responsible for maintaining backups of their personal expense data.

IMPORTANT: Personal expense data lives exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the App results in permanent, unrecoverable loss of all personal expense data. There is no automatic backup or cloud recovery mechanism for personal expense data at this time.

4.2 Group Expense Data

Group expense data refers to all expense entries, settlement records, and transaction logs created within a group managed on the SyncSpend platform.

  • Group expense data is stored securely in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, our third-party cloud infrastructure provider, and is also cached locally on your device as described in Section 3.5. See Section 8.2 for how these two copies relate and how long each is retained.
  • The cloud database serves as the authoritative source of truth for all group expense data and is used to synchronize data across devices of group members.
  • Group expense data is accessible to the group owner and authorised group members as described in Section 6 of this Policy.
  • Group expense data is subject to data retention limits based on the user's subscription tier as described in Section 8.

4.3 Cloud as Source of Truth

For group features, the SyncSpend cloud infrastructure is the definitive, authoritative record of all group transactions. In the event of a discrepancy between data on a user's device and data on the cloud, the cloud record shall prevail. Users must maintain an active subscription to retain access to group cloud data beyond the applicable free retention period.

6. Group Data Ownership, Member Rights, and Access Control

6.1 Nature of Group Expense Data

SyncSpend's group feature is designed as an expense logging tool for funds administered by the group owner. Expense entries logged within a group represent records of expenditures made from funds under the group owner's administration. Members who log entries within a group are recording how the group owner's administered funds were spent on behalf of the group.

Group expense entries may contain personal data contributed by members. Such data is processed jointly for the purpose of shared financial record-keeping. Users acknowledge that participation in a group involves shared data control between the group owner and members, within the boundaries described in this Section.

Accordingly, the group owner holds primary administrative rights over group expense records, and member rights within a group are limited as described in this Section.

6.2 Group Owner Rights

  • The group owner has the exclusive right to create, manage, and permanently delete the group.
  • The group owner can view all expense entries logged by all current and former members within their groups.
  • The group owner can add or remove members from the group at any time.
  • When a group owner permanently deletes a group, all associated group expense data, transaction logs, and member records are permanently and irrecoverably erased from both cloud servers and any locally cached copies on member devices. This action cannot be undone.

6.3 Member Rights and Limitations Within Groups

  • Group members can log expense entries within the group and view group expense records as permitted by the group owner.
  • A member may edit or delete their most recently logged expense entry within a group, provided no subsequent expense entry has been logged by any member after their entry. Once a subsequent entry exists, the earlier entry becomes immutable and cannot be edited or deleted by the member. This restriction exists to preserve the integrity of the sequential financial record.
  • Members do not have the right to delete or modify other members' expense entries.
  • Regardless of subscription tier, only expense entries logged within the current calendar month are editable by the member who created them, subject to the sequential immutability rule above. Expense entries from all prior calendar months are permanently locked and read-only to preserve the integrity of the sequential financial record. This rule applies to all members including the group owner.

In-app notification of the entry immutability rule will be displayed to users within the group interface. Please read in-app guidance carefully before logging entries.

6.4 Data Upon Member Removal

When a member is removed from a group by the group owner:

  • The removed member immediately loses the ability to view, access, edit, or add any data within that group.
  • Expense entries previously logged by the removed member remain within the group record as part of the permanent financial log. This data is retained to preserve the integrity of the group's historical financial record, which the group owner is entitled to maintain.
  • The display name associated with the removed member's entries is retained as it appeared at the time of logging. This is necessary to preserve record accuracy across historical transaction logs. Retroactive name changes across historical records are not technically supported in order to maintain data integrity.

This retention of historical entries by a removed member is justified under the legitimate interest of maintaining accurate financial records by the group owner, in accordance with GDPR Article 6(1)(f) and applicable data protection law.

6.5 GDPR Right to Erasure in Group Context

Where a removed member submits a valid Right to Erasure request under GDPR or applicable law, GreenLeafTech will assess such requests on a case-by-case basis. We reserve the right to decline erasure of group expense entries where erasure would materially impair the financial record-keeping rights of the group owner, consistent with the legitimate interest exception under applicable data protection legislation. Requests may be submitted to greenleaftech.co.in@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

SyncSpend follows a data minimisation approach. We retain data only as long as necessary to deliver our services and fulfil legitimate business and legal purposes.

8.1 Personal Expense Data

  • Stored exclusively on the user's device and fully under the user's control.
  • Not transmitted to or retained by GreenLeafTech servers unless cloud features are explicitly enabled.
  • Permanently deleted upon uninstallation of the App from the device. GreenLeafTech has no ability to recover this data.

8.2 Group Expense Data Retention

  • Group expense data is stored locally on each member's device indefinitely, for as long as the App remains installed, regardless of any cloud retention period described below. You can access or export this data from your device at any time.
  • Separately, a copy of group expense data is also retained in cloud storage. This cloud copy serves two purposes: (1) allowing you to recover your data if you lose your device, reinstall the App, or sign in on a new device, and (2) allowing existing group data to be shown to a member who newly joins the group. The cloud copy is not the primary storage location for your data.
  • The cloud copy is retained for up to 2 (two) years from the date of entry, for all users regardless of subscription tier. Upon expiry of this period, the relevant data is permanently deleted from cloud servers only — your locally stored copy is unaffected.
  • Independently of the above, if a group has no activity of any kind for 6 (six) consecutive months — meaning no new entries, no member additions or removals, and no member has opened the group — the group's cloud copy is considered dormant and is permanently deleted from cloud servers, regardless of how much of the 2-year window remains. This does not affect any member's locally stored copy of that group's data.
  • Once deleted from cloud servers under either rule above, that data cannot be recovered from the cloud. If you still have the App installed on the device where you originally used the group, your local copy remains unaffected and can still be exported at any time.

8.3 Account Deletion

Upon a user's request for account deletion:

  • Personal profile data and other account-associated data that GreenLeafTech is required to delete will be permanently deleted from cloud servers following an account deletion request. Certain historical group expense records created by the user may remain where necessary to preserve the group's shared financial record, as described in Section 6.4, and subject to applicable law.
  • The user's email address is retained for a period of 12 (twelve) months following account deletion. This retention is necessary for the sole purpose of identifying whether the email address was previously associated with a free trial, thereby preventing fraudulent reuse of the trial feature. This is a legitimate business interest under applicable data protection law. Users may object to this processing under applicable law by contacting us at greenleaftech.co.in@gmail.com. Such objections will be assessed against our legitimate interest in preventing trial abuse, and we will respond within 30 days.
  • After the 12-month retention period, the email address is permanently and irrecoverably deleted.
  • Expense entries previously logged by the deleted account within active groups are governed by Section 6.4 of this Policy.

8.4 Group Deletion by Owner

When a group owner permanently deletes a group:

  • All group expense data, transaction logs, member records, and associated metadata are permanently and irrecoverably erased from cloud servers.
  • Any locally cached group data on member devices is also cleared upon next synchronisation.
  • This action is final and irreversible. The group owner is solely responsible for exporting or recording any data they wish to retain before deleting the group.

8.5 No Automatic Backup

SyncSpend does not currently provide automatic data backup for personal expense data. User-initiated data export is available as described in Section 10. Users are solely responsible for maintaining their own records until such time as an automated backup feature is made available, which will be disclosed via an updated Policy.

12. AlertLog — Transaction Alert Detection

AlertLog is a separate, standalone feature of SyncSpend, distinct from the personal and group expense tracking described in Sections 3.2 and 4. It is not a variant, mode, or extension of those features, and data handled by AlertLog is not governed by Sections 3.2 or 4 unless expressly stated in this Section. AlertLog helps you log expenses automatically by detecting transaction-related alerts shown in your device's notification shade (for example, payment or transfer alerts from banking apps, UPI apps, or other apps you use). Where anything in this Section conflicts with a general statement made elsewhere in this Policy regarding notifications, SMS, or communications, this Section governs for the AlertLog feature.

12.1 How AlertLog Works

  • AlertLog is disabled by default and only activates after you're shown an in-app explanation and affirmatively grant Android's Notification Access permission.
  • Once enabled, AlertLog reads notification text via Android's Notification Listener capability, and a keyword-based filter discards anything that doesn't contain transaction-related language (for example, terms associated with debits, credits, transfers, or payments); non-matching content is not retained or processed further.
  • Matching notifications are parsed on-device to extract structured details such as amount and transaction type, and placed into an in-app review inbox. No entry is created automatically — you must manually confirm or delete each item, and deleted items are not stored.
  • Confirmed entries are saved into a dedicated, on-device AlertLog store, kept separate from your personal and group expense records described in Sections 3.2 and 4.
  • Detection quality depends on the notification's content and format. Some apps or device manufacturers may truncate, summarize, or obscure notification text before AlertLog can read it, in which case an entry may be incomplete or missed entirely.

12.2 Data Stays On Your Device

  • All AlertLog data — detected notification text, parsed transaction details, and confirmed entries — is stored exclusively in local storage on your device. It is never transmitted to GreenLeafTech's servers, Firebase, Firestore, or any other cloud infrastructure used by SyncSpend.
  • AlertLog entries are never visible to other users, including members of any group you belong to elsewhere in the App. AlertLog does not sync or share data with group members under any circumstance.
  • AlertLog data is not included in any automatic device or cloud backup performed by SyncSpend. The only way AlertLog data leaves your device is if you manually use the App's export feature to generate an Excel or PDF file, which is created and saved to your device (or shared by you) at your own initiative — see Section 10 (Data Export).
  • If AlertLog data is included in your device's own operating-system-level backup (for example, a general Android device backup you configure yourself outside the App), that is governed by your device manufacturer's or Android's backup policy, not by SyncSpend or this Policy.
  • GreenLeafTech does not sell, share, transmit, or use AlertLog-derived data for advertising, marketing, profiling, analytics, or any purpose whatsoever, because GreenLeafTech has no access to this data — it never leaves your device except through your own manual export action.
  • AlertLog data (notification content, parsed details, and confirmed entries) is never sent to Firebase Crashlytics, RevenueCat, any analytics or crash-reporting pipeline, or any other third-party SDK integrated into the App.

12.3 Retention of Intermediate Data

  • When a notification matches AlertLog's keyword filter, its text is temporarily processed on-device before being turned into a transaction entry. This intermediate data is automatically deleted within 3 days regardless of whether it was successfully processed, so it does not accumulate indefinitely.
  • Separately, AlertLog may detect that an app (such as your device's default messaging app) is not showing readable notification content — meaning a message may be hidden from AlertLog by a device setting — and keeps a diagnostic record of that event on-device for up to 30 days. If this happens repeatedly for the same app within a short period, the App shows you an in-app notice suggesting you check that app's notification visibility settings. This diagnostic record is used only for this purpose and is automatically deleted after 30 days.

12.4 Scope of Access and What Is Not Done

  • AlertLog does not request or use the READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS Android permissions. It does not read your SMS inbox directly.
  • Because Android's Notification Listener capability grants access to the content of all notifications on the device, AlertLog technically has the ability to read notifications from apps unrelated to finance. The on-device keyword filter described in Section 12.1 is designed to immediately discard such content, and GreenLeafTech does not use, store, or review non-financial notification content.
  • AlertLog does not access notification content while the permission is turned off in your device settings or in the in-app AlertLog toggle described below.
  • The App may query which app is set as your device's default SMS handler, solely to support the in-app "message visibility" guidance that helps you check your notification settings if needed. This query never reads the content of your SMS messages and does not use the READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS permissions.
