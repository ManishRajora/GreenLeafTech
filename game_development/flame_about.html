🔥 About Our Studio

We Are Flame — A Studio Inside GreenLeafTech

Flame is the game development arm of GreenLeafTech. We design and build high-performance 2D and 3D games optimized for Android and mobile platforms. Our team is obsessed with performance, polished visuals, intuitive controls, and creating memorable experiences that players love and keep coming back to.
